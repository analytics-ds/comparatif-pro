> **Key points:**
> 1. In Extenso is a traditional accounting firm (1991, 250 branches, 7,500 employees), French leader for SMEs and small businesses, with a dedicated advisor per client and a full-service offer (accounting, tax, legal, succession).
> 2. Pennylane is a SaaS financial management platform coupled with a network of partner firms, optimised for automation, bank synchronisation and digital self-management.
> 3. In Extenso wins on human advice, growth support and local proximity. Pennylane wins on ease of use, tool modernity and low entry cost.
> 4. The verdict: In Extenso if you seek a growth partner and advisor; Pennylane if you seek financial management software with accounting support in the background.

In Extenso and Pennylane embody two diametrically opposed models of accounting expertise in France. One is built on the traditional firm with a dedicated advisor and multi-service offerings; the other on a digital platform backed by a network of partner firms. Which to choose? This comparison details the differences, advantages and use cases of each.

## In Extenso: the historic firm with a dedicated advisor

In Extenso was founded in 1991 by former Deloitte partners. Thirty-five years later, it is the French leader in accounting expertise for SMEs and small businesses, with near-national coverage. Its 7,500 employees spread across 250 branches make it a dense proximity network, a major strength against purely digital competitors.

**The service scope.** In Extenso sells a full-service offer: statutory accounting (99% of revenue), integrated optional services (tax, payroll, legal, business creation, succession support, management reporting and business advice). This breadth means an entrepreneur can entrust a single contact with accounting, VAT return, tax audit, director contracts, succession questions, and even restructuring projects. This range appeals to growing SMEs.

**The business model.** In Extenso typically charges a fixed monthly flat fee (250 to 300 euros for a small business, negotiable based on workload). No surprise costs. You pay for an advisory service, not just bookkeeping. Your advisor is identified, you can call them, ask business questions beyond pure accounting.

**The user experience.** You do not manage your own dashboard. You submit invoices and supporting documents (paper, email, secure portal depending on the branch). The firm processes, performs controls, tax adjustments, and provides you with a complete set of documents monthly or quarterly. It is passive: you delegate, the firm manages. For some, this is a comfort; for others, a lack of responsiveness.

## Pennylane: the SaaS platform with strong automation

Pennylane was founded in 2013 by founders Sophie Viger and Milo Krémastiotis. It is a pure SaaS platform: accounting and financial management software plus a backed accounting infrastructure. Pennylane sells a modern UX, automation and the ability to self-manage.

**The service scope.** Pennylane natively integrates: accounting management, automatic bank synchronisation, electronic invoicing (native PDP), real-time financial reporting, digital archiving, and a network of partner firms (around thirty approved firms) to validate accounting and sign annual returns. No legal or advanced tax offering. You manage 80% of tracking; the partner firm validates and signs annually.

**The business model.** Pennylane charges per usage: base subscription (29 euros/month for very small micro-business), then optional modules. An average SME user counts between 80 and 150 euros per month. Pay-as-you-grow: you pay by invoice and transaction volume. No flat fee, more pricing flexibility.

**The user experience.** Modern and intuitive dashboard. You connect your bank account (Open Banking), invoices sync, entries auto-generate. You manage your cash flow in real-time, see your ratios, anticipate. Pennylane automates most repetitive tasks. In return, you must learn the tool, maintain good entry practices (correct accounting labels, consistent categories). The partner firm mainly handles balance sheet signature and pre-filing tax advice.

## When to choose In Extenso?

**Entrepreneurs in growth.** If you face restructuring issues (subsidiary creation, acquisition, tax regime change, future succession), In Extenso offers a complete ecosystem. Your advisor can propose scenarios, help you gain tax points, support you through major changes.

**Multi-site and accounting complexity.** Multiple locations, multiple currencies, different tax regimes. In Extenso masters these cases; Pennylane remains oriented toward simple small structures. An In Extenso advisor will hold your hand.

**Need for local proximity.** You want a firm near you, able to discuss face-to-face with someone who knows your industry and local ecosystem. In Extenso's 250 branches cover almost all French postcodes.

**Support in legal, payroll, succession.** In Extenso offers these services under one roof (or via in-house partners). You manage a single relationship. With Pennylane, you must find another provider.

## When to choose Pennylane?

**Self-managed and digital.** You are comfortable with software interfaces, you like to control, see figures in real-time. Pennylane hands you the keys. You decide when to issue an invoice, when to record an entry, and your balance sheet updates instantly.

**Small budget or startup.** Pennylane costs less upfront (29 euros per month versus 250+). If your revenue is low or you are testing a model, Pennylane makes sense. In Extenso imposes a minimum flat fee even if your activity is micro.

**Real-time financial management.** You need to know your cash flow every day, your ratios, profitability by customer or project. Pennylane offers a rich dashboard. In Extenso provides you reports; Pennylane gives you the tool to build them yourself.

**Simplified bank synchronisation.** Open Banking, connect once, everything syncs. It is a huge productivity gain. In Extenso offers it too, but less integrated. Pennylane made it its core.

**Mandatory electronic invoicing.** Pennylane natively integrates PDP transmission. It is transparent for you. In Extenso relies on partners (Cegid Pulse, etc.), more links in the chain.

## FAQ

**Q: In Extenso or Pennylane for a freelancer?**
A: Pennylane. A freelancer is simple, little accounting volume. You would self-manage without needing a dedicated advisor. Pennylane gives you a powerful tool at low cost. In Extenso imposes a flat fee you would not break even on.

**Q: Is Pennylane secure for sensitive data?**
A: Yes. Pennylane is ISO 27001, hosted in the European Union, GDPR compliant. Bank and accounting data are encrypted in transit and at rest. No security difference from a traditional firm.

**Q: Can you switch from Pennylane to In Extenso (or vice-versa) without data loss?**
A: Yes. Both offer structured exports (FEC - Accounting Entry File, balance sheets, trial balances). Migration takes a few weeks of synchronisation. No proprietary lock-in.

## Verdict

The In Extenso or Pennylane choice is not a quality duel, but an arbitrage between two complementary models.

**Choose In Extenso** if you seek a growth partner, if your business is becoming complex (multi-site, legal, succession, restructuring), or if you prefer to fully delegate to a human advisor. You pay more, you gain an ecosystem, proximity and field expertise.

**Choose Pennylane** if you seek a modern, performant accounting software, if you like to drive, and if your structure remains simple. You pay less, you gain autonomy and real-time financial transparency.

Many entrepreneurs use both: Pennylane daily for tracking, In Extenso once a year for expertise and signature. This is a viable hybrid model.
