## What is quote software and why equip your business with it

**Quote software** lets you create, send and track priced commercial proposals before they are converted into an invoice. It replaces spreadsheet or word-processor quote templates, which are prone to calculation errors, inconsistent numbering and poorly tracked follow-ups.

For a small business, an SME or a contractor, the quote is the first contractual document sent to a client. Its presentation quality, the speed it is issued and its compliance (legal notices, VAT, terms and conditions) directly affect the signature rate and the professional image of the business.

### A contractual document that should not be neglected

An accepted quote legally commits both parties to the price, deadlines and scope of the service. A calculation error or a missing notice can lead to a dispute or a loss of margin. Quote software reduces this risk by automating VAT, discount and total calculations, and by keeping a dated history of every version sent to the client.

## The criteria for choosing the right quote solution

The choice of this tool depends on the industry, the volume of quotes issued and the level of integration wanted with invoicing and accounting. A business that issues a few quotes a month does not have the same requirements as a construction company pricing several a week across different job sites.

### The features that make the difference

- **Automatic conversion of a quote into an invoice**: an accepted quote should become an invoice without re-entering line items, quantities and prices.
- **Electronic signature and online sending**: these features shorten the delay between sending the quote and its acceptance by the client.
- **A library of services or work items**: essential in construction to price quickly from preconfigured catalogues.
- **Customisable templates**: logo, layout and legal notices matching the business's image.
- **Automatic follow-ups**: tracking quotes awaiting a response, to limit proposals left without an answer.

## Focus on business profiles: general small business or construction contractor

A service-based small business (consulting, intellectual services, retail) does not have the same needs as a construction company. The former favours integrating quotes into a sales pipeline, an issue covered in our [CRM software comparison](/en/blog/best-crm-software/), and into invoicing, which Axonaut and Sellsy offer. The latter needs fast pricing from catalogues of services and materials, a strength of Obat and Tolteck.

This speed of pricing and signing is not just a convenience. A Banque de France study published in 2022 showed that payment delays on invoices increase a company's probability of failure:

> "Payment delays increase a company's probability of failure by 25 percent, and by 40 percent if the delays exceed one month."
> — Banque de France, 2022

A quote signed quickly, converted into an invoice without delay, shortens the cycle between the client's commitment and the cash actually being collected, a direct cash flow issue for small businesses and SMEs.

## How to move efficiently from quote to invoice

1. **Choose a clear quote template**: contact details, a detailed description of the service, prices excl. and incl. VAT, and validity terms.
2. **Send the quote online with electronic signature** to reduce the acceptance delay, a feature available at Axonaut, Obat and Tolteck and detailed in our [electronic signature software comparison](/en/blog/electronic-signature-software/).
3. **Convert the accepted quote into an invoice in one click**, without re-entering line items, to eliminate copying errors.
4. **Schedule an automatic follow-up** on quotes left without a response beyond a set delay.
5. **Check the compliance of the invoice issued** with current e-invoicing obligations, a point to cross-reference with our [e-invoicing software comparison](/en/blog/best-electronic-invoicing-software/).
6. **Archive each quote and its linked invoice** to quickly retrieve a client's history in case of a dispute or an audit, a feature natively offered by all five solutions compared here.

This sequence of steps reduces the number of manual tasks between sending the commercial proposal and collecting payment, which limits copying errors and missed follow-ups, two frequent causes of late payment for small businesses and SMEs.
