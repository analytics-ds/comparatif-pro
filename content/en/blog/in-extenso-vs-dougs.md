**In Extenso or Dougs?** The question faces every small business since online accounting firms arrived. Dougs represents modernity, price transparency and automation for freelancers and micro-businesses. In Extenso stands for local roots, a dedicated advisor and advisory services that last. The choice depends on your company profile and growth ambitions.

This comparison reviews the strengths of each solution and helps you decide based on your business stage and complexity.

> **In short:**
> 1. Dougs targets freelancers, micro-businesses and simple small businesses that want transparent pricing and a decentralised app. In Extenso targets growing small businesses anticipating advice beyond accounting compliance.
> 2. Dougs starts from around 49 euros per month, In Extenso is between 150 and 300 euros per month, depending on scope.
> 3. In Extenso offers a dedicated advisor in an office, national coverage of more than 230 offices and access to cross-cutting expertise. Dougs automates data entry and delivers an integrated app, without human support.
> 4. For a small business that remains simple and self-sufficient, Dougs is enough. For a small business that is growing, hiring or planning a transfer, In Extenso secures the journey without changing partner.

## Two philosophies, two paths

### Dougs: automation and low cost for a stable small business

Dougs was founded on a simple premise: bookkeeping for a micro-business or simple small business with no staff does not need a dedicated advisor, it needs a robust app and clear pricing. The platform connects to the bank account, automatically declares transactions and offers dashboards to track turnover and spending. No price surprises, no calls for extra advice.

This approach works all the better as your activity stays simple. A freelancer, a start-up tradesperson, a service small business with no special payroll charges finds in Dougs a very controlled cost base. That is precisely what makes Dougs attractive to micro-entrepreneurs and the self-employed. Read our analysis of [online accountant versus a traditional firm](/en/blog/online-accountant-vs-traditional-firm/) to dig deeper into this choice.

The limit of Dougs appears as soon as your activity moves beyond this simple scope. As soon as you hire a first employee, you look for a way to optimise your salary or you plan to borrow, Dougs no longer offers the advice you need. This is when many Dougs clients discover they need to change firms, risking loss of accounting history and continuity.

### In Extenso: the advice that grows with you

In Extenso operates on different logic. Founded in 1991 and owned by its partner chartered accountants, it has built a national network of more than 230 offices and 7,300 staff. The goal: to be the sole partner of a company, from its creation through to transfer or multi-site expansion. To do this, In Extenso assigns a dedicated advisor in a local office who knows your file, your challenges and your sector, and can work on bookkeeping, tax, payroll, start-up advice, performance management and support for structuring operations.

This approach costs more, between 150 and 300 euros per month, but it secures your growth journey. You do not need to look for a new firm as soon as your business grows more complex. Your advisor evolves with you and calls on the network's full expertise if needed.

## How to choose based on your profile

### You are a start-up and want to minimise costs

If you are a sole trader or have just set up a simple limited company with no planned staff, Dougs is right for you. Pricing is transparent, the platform is intuitive and bank automation saves you time. You will have a clear picture of your accounts with no hidden fees.

### You have a stable small business for several years

If your activity is settled, you do not plan significant hiring and you only need advice on tax and payroll returns, Dougs continues to serve you well. The controlled cost remains an asset, and you do not need a dedicated contact.

### You are planning growth or hiring

As soon as you plan to hire, expand across several areas or negotiate a loan, switch to In Extenso. A dedicated advisor who knows your file saves huge time and avoids costly mistakes. To better prepare this choice, read our [guide on how to choose a chartered accountant](/en/blog/how-to-choose-chartered-accountant/).

### You need specialist sector expertise

In Extenso covers all sectors, with teams specialised in trades, professional services, retail, hospitality and the social economy. If your sector requires fine tax or payroll expertise, that is a major advantage. Dougs offers a generic approach.

## Our verdict

**In Extenso** wins the comparison for a small business that is planning ahead. It combines the proximity of a dedicated advisor in a local office, a national network of more than 230 offices and a full advisory scope without partner changes.

**Dougs** wins the comparison for a simple, self-sufficient small business that wants low cost and price clarity. The modern app and automated entry delight freelancers and micro-businesses.

Be honest about your growth prospects over the next two to three years. If you are aiming for stability and cost control, Dougs is lighter. If you are building towards growth, an SME or a transfer, In Extenso offers the coverage and durability that last.
