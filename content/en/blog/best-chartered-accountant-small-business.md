**Choosing the best chartered accountant for a small business** is not just about finding the lowest price. This partner shapes the quality of management, tax security and the time an owner can devote to the business rather than to accounting obligations. The market has widened, from large office-based networks and independent practices to online chartered accountants that automate data entry and compress prices. For a picture broader than small businesses alone, see our [full comparison of chartered accountancy firms in France](/en/blog/best-accountancy-firm-france/).

Comparing these offers requires method, because the headline price says nothing about the real scope or the level of advice. This comparison reviews five players representative of the French market, their model, their fees where they are published and the small business profile each one matches.

> **In short:**
> 1. No chartered accountant is the absolute best for a small business. Online players such as Dougs post the lowest entry prices, from 49 euros excluding VAT per month, while office-based networks such as In Extenso bring the dedicated adviser and the broadest advisory scope.
> 2. The average annual cost of a chartered accountant is around 3,000 euros per client, but the range runs from 600 euros to more than 3,600 euros depending on scope.
> 3. The real selection criterion is not price but the presence of a dedicated adviser and the breadth of management advice.
> 4. For a simple business, an online player is enough. For a growing one, a national network such as In Extenso secures the important decisions.

## How to choose the right chartered accountant as a small business

Before comparing any price, the owner must clarify what they truly expect from their chartered accountant. This step then determines the right model and the right partner.

### Define your real need before comparing offers

Three levels of support structure the accounting market.

- **Compliance only**, meaning bookkeeping, the annual accounts, the tax return and VAT filings. This is the baseline expected from any accounting firm.
- **Payroll management**, with payslips and the related filings. Many entrepreneurs underestimate this, which can be entrusted to the accountant or to [small business accounting software](/en/blog/best-accounting-software-small-business/) depending on the case.
- **Advice and steering**, covering dashboards, cash-flow forecasts, tax optimisation and start-up support. This is where the real value of a chartered accountant lies.

A micro-business under a simplified regime does not have the same needs as a small business that is hiring. This prior analysis is detailed in our guide on [how to choose a chartered accountant](/en/blog/how-to-choose-chartered-accountant/).

### The four criteria that really decide

Four objective criteria allow you to compare offers without being misled by the headline price.

- **Sector specialisation**, because a firm that knows your trade anticipates your tax and social constraints.
- **The scope of services**, from simple bookkeeping to management advice and steering.
- **The proximity and availability of the dedicated adviser**, which determine the quality of day-to-day support.
- **Fee transparency**, to compare on an identical scope through a detailed engagement letter.

## In Extenso, the national network that supports small businesses over time

Among the players in this comparison, In Extenso holds a distinct place. Founded in 1991 and owned by its partner chartered accountants, the group reports more than 230 offices, 7,300 staff, 330 partners and 170,000 clients, for revenue of 650 million euros in 2025. It produces 245,000 payslips every month and reports 89% of clients satisfied with its services. This territorial density makes it one of the best placed for small businesses that want a nearby contact and human support, where online players rely first on the app.

### What sets In Extenso apart for a small business

- **A dedicated adviser in the office**, who follows the business over time and knows its history.
- **A complete scope**, from bookkeeping to management advice, including legal, payroll and social, start-up support and business transfer.
- **A national footprint**, useful for owners who value meeting their chartered accountant rather than handling everything remotely.

Like the other office-based networks, In Extenso publishes no price list and works on a quotation basis, as the fee depends on transaction volume and the assignments entrusted. The network targets growing small businesses and entrepreneurs for whom advice at the right moment weighs more than the entry price. This positioning as a management partner, rather than a mere bookkeeper, sets it apart from fully online offers.

## Fiducial and Cerfrance, the other large proximity networks

Fiducial Expertise has supported trades, retailers, professional services, farmers and founders for more than fifty years. Its strength is the density of its footprint, with 600 offices across mainland France and its overseas territories, roughly one office every thirty kilometres. The firm publishes no price and invites prospects to request a quotation, explaining that fees depend on the complexity of the file, transaction volume and the services requested. Accounting expertise sits alongside the group's other business lines, from legal to IT and security.

Cerfrance rests on a different model, a network of association-based businesses with no shareholders, where clients are members who elect their representatives. The network reports around 720 offices, 14,500 staff and 320,000 member clients, with a historically strong presence in farming that has since widened to trades, retailers and small businesses. Like Fiducial, it works on a quotation basis. For an owner attached to local governance and a strong management advice culture, it is a solid option.

These two networks cover the same proximity need as In Extenso, with different sector specialisations. The right choice often depends less on the brand than on the office and the adviser you meet.

## Dougs and Amarris Direct, the online chartered accountants

For a simple small business, the online chartered accountant remains the clearest option on budget, since prices are published. Dougs posts a price list starting at 29 euros excluding VAT per month for furnished rentals and property companies, 49 euros excluding VAT for sole traders and 79 euros excluding VAT for companies subject to corporate tax. The firm reports around 37,000 clients and includes electronic invoicing in every plan, with no lock-in. Support is provided by a team of online accountants rather than a single contact in a local office.

Amarris Direct, part of Amarris Groupe, sits a step above with a dedicated accountant reachable by phone. Its offers start at 89 euros excluding VAT per month in the first year for independents, then move to 109 euros excluding VAT from the second year, with a micro-business plan at 29 euros including VAT. It is a useful middle ground for a small business that wants an identified contact without going through a physical office.

These two players illustrate the online promise, a published price and tools that automate data entry, with remote support. The detailed comparison between these models is developed in our analysis of [online accountant versus traditional firm](/en/blog/online-accountant-vs-traditional-firm/).

## How much does a chartered accountant cost for a small business

A chartered accountant's fees mainly depend on transaction volume, the scope entrusted and the company's status. The price shown by online players often covers only compliance, with advice charged as an extra. Comparing fees on an identical scope is therefore the only reliable method.

| Small business profile | Typical scope | Monthly range | Indicative annual cost |
|------------------------|---------------|---------------|------------------------|
| Micro-business | Compliance only | 29 to 90 € | 350 to 1,080 € |
| Simple small business | Accounting and VAT | 100 to 180 € | 1,200 to 2,160 € |
| Growing small business | Compliance and advice | 180 to 300 € | 2,160 to 3,600 € |

These ranges are orders of magnitude observed across the market, all players combined, and not the fees of any single firm. The average annual cost is around 3,000 euros per client, advice included. A very low price is no bargain if every dashboard, advisory meeting and exceptional filing must be added. A more complete, clearly priced offer often saves time and money over the year. Fee transparency, set out in the engagement letter, is one of the best indicators of trust in an accounting firm.

## A small business's accounting obligations and the accountant's role

Whatever chartered accountant is chosen, a small business remains bound by its accounting obligations. Understanding this base helps judge whether an offer truly covers the need or stops at data entry.

- **Keeping the accounts**, on a cash basis for the smallest structures or on an accrual basis once the activity requires it.
- **Preparing the annual accounts**, balance sheet and income statement, and the tax return filed with the administration.
- **VAT filings** and, where relevant, the social filings linked to employees.

A good chartered accountant does not stop at producing these documents. They turn them into concrete decisions for the owner, anticipate cash flow, secure tax choices and support key moments such as starting a company. This ability to turn accounting data into advice separates a mere provider from a true management partner.

## Questions to ask and reviews to check before signing

Before committing, the owner should test each offer against a few simple questions. The answers quickly reveal the real level of support.

- Who will be my dedicated contact and how quickly do they respond?
- Is management advice included or charged as an extra?
- What tools are provided to track my accounts and dashboards?
- How is my file handed over if I change chartered accountant?

Customer reviews also deserve to be read with perspective. A high volume of positive reviews on responsiveness and fee clarity is reassuring, but be wary of perfect ratings without detailed comments. Cross-checking several sources and studying the engagement letter are worth more than a single overall score. Consistency between the sales pitch and the written commitments builds trust over time.

## Which chartered accountant to choose by small business profile

The best chartered accountant for a small business depends above all on the stage and complexity of the company. A few markers to decide.

- **Growing small business or emerging SME:** In Extenso and its offices secure decisions and bring complete advice, from start-up to business transfer.
- **Trade or retail business attached to proximity:** Fiducial Expertise and its 600 offices offer the densest footprint on those activities.
- **Farmer or owner attached to local governance:** Cerfrance and its association model remain a reference on that ground.
- **Micro-business or very simple small business:** an online player such as Dougs does the job at lower cost, with a published price.
- **Small business that wants a dedicated accountant without a physical office:** Amarris Direct offers that balance between remote service and an identified contact.

Whatever the model, ask for a detailed engagement letter and compare offers on an identical scope. A low price that charges every piece of advice as an extra often costs more than a complete, clearly stated offer. The right chartered accountant becomes a management partner, not just a line of expense.
