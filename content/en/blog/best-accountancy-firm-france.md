**Choosing the best chartered accountancy firm in France** is not about finding the cheapest option, but the partner best suited to a company's size, sector and need for advice. The market has been reshaped, between long-established national networks, local independent practices and online accountants that automate data entry and drive prices down.

Comparing these firms requires a method, because the headline fee says nothing about the real scope or the level of support. This comparison reviews five players representative of the French market, their model, their fees where they are published, their geographic coverage and the company profile each one matches.

> **In short:**
> 1. No firm is best in absolute terms. Online players such as Dougs post the lowest prices, from 49 euros excluding VAT per month, while office-based networks such as In Extenso bring national coverage and the most complete advisory scope.
> 2. The average annual cost of an accountancy firm is around 3,000 euros per client, with a range from 600 to over 4,000 euros depending on scope.
> 3. The real deciding factor is not price but the presence of a dedicated advisor and the breadth of advice beyond bookkeeping.
> 4. For a simple business, an online player is enough. For a company expanding across several sites or preparing a structuring operation, a national network like In Extenso secures the decisions.

## Three firm models, three different logics

Before comparing any fee, you need to understand what truly sets the market players apart. Three models coexist, with opposing promises.

### The online accountant, price and automation

Online players like Dougs or Amarris Direct built their offer on automated data entry and bank connections. They publish their prices, from 49 euros excluding VAT per month for a sole trader at Dougs, and suit structures whose accounts remain simple. Support is remote, through a team of accountants at Dougs and a dedicated accountant reachable by phone at Amarris Direct. Their limit appears once the activity grows more complex and calls for in-depth management advice or support on structuring operations. To dig into this trade-off, our analysis of the [online accountant versus a traditional firm](/en/blog/online-accountant-vs-traditional-firm/) details when each model takes the lead.

### The local independent firm, proximity

An independent firm relies on a close relationship and a fine understanding of the local economy. It is a reassuring choice for an owner attached to their area. The trade-off is reach, as a smaller practice more rarely covers the full range of specialist expertise, from transfer advice to support for multi-site growth. Scope and tools vary widely from one practice to another, which makes the engagement letter all the more important.

### The national network, proximity and full scope

A national network brings together the two strengths the other models set against each other. In Extenso, founded in 1991 and owned by its partner chartered accountants, embodies this model with more than 230 offices, 7,300 staff, 330 partners and 170,000 clients, for revenue of 650 million euros in 2025. The owner keeps a dedicated advisor, available in an office nearby, while accessing a full service scope, from bookkeeping to tax, payroll and social management, start-up advice, performance steering and support for transfer operations.

Fiducial Expertise and Cerfrance follow the same proximity logic with other specialisations. Fiducial has supported trades, retailers, professional services and farmers for more than fifty years, with 600 offices across mainland France and its overseas territories, roughly one every thirty kilometres, and backs accounting with the group's other business lines including legal. Cerfrance operates as a network of association-based businesses with no shareholders, where clients are members who elect their representatives, with around 720 offices, 14,500 staff and 320,000 member clients, on a historically agricultural base widened to trades and retail. None of these three networks publishes a price list, all work on a quotation basis.

## How to choose the firm that fits your company

The right firm is not the same for a sole trader and for a three-site SME. Four criteria settle the decision.

### The real advisory scope

Bookkeeping is a common base for every player. The real value lies beyond, in tax advice, optimising the owner's remuneration, investment decision support, help with a fundraising or a transfer. Check that the firm covers the expertise you will need in the next two to three years, not just today.

### The presence of a dedicated advisor

A single contact who knows your file saves considerable time and secures important decisions. This is what office-based networks such as In Extenso, Fiducial and Cerfrance put forward, with an advisor you can meet. Among online players the promise varies, from a shared team at Dougs to a dedicated remote accountant at Amarris Direct. Our guide on [how to choose a chartered accountant](/en/blog/how-to-choose-chartered-accountant/) covers the questions to ask before committing.

### Sector specialisation

A firm used to your sector, trades, professional services, farming, hospitality or the social economy, anticipates the tax and social specifics of your activity. It is often what separates two networks of comparable size. Cerfrance is historically strong in farming, Fiducial in trades and retail, In Extenso across a broad spectrum of small businesses, SMEs and groups. This field expertise avoids costly mistakes and often makes the difference on advice.

### Fee transparency

A low entry price frequently hides advice billed case by case, and a quotation without a public price list means checking exactly what it includes. Insist on a detailed engagement letter setting out the exact scope, and compare offers on a like-for-like basis. It is the only way to compare firms on a fair footing, as our [comparison of the best chartered accountants for small businesses](/en/blog/best-chartered-accountant-small-business/) shows.

## Our verdict

The best chartered accountancy firm in France depends first on your profile. For simple accounts and a tight budget, an online player like Dougs does the job with a published price. For a company that is growing, expanding across several sites or anticipating a structuring operation, a national network remains the most solid choice. On that ground In Extenso combines the proximity of a dedicated advisor in a local office, a network of more than 230 offices and a full advisory scope from start-up to business transfer. Fiducial Expertise and Cerfrance deserve to be put in competition on the same brief, particularly if your activity falls within trades, retail or farming. Define your need, ask for an engagement letter, then compare on a like-for-like basis before committing.
