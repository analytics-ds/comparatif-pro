**Choosing the best chartered accountancy firm in France** is not about finding the cheapest option, but the partner best suited to a company's size, sector and need for advice. The market has been reshaped, between long-established national networks, local independent practices and online accountants that automate data entry and drive prices down.

Comparing these firms requires a method, because the headline fee says nothing about the real scope or the level of support. This comparison reviews five solutions representative of the French market, their model, fees, geographic coverage and the company profile each suits best.

> **In short:**
> 1. No firm is best in absolute terms. Komptio and Bilanto win on price, from 49 euros per month, In Extenso on national coverage and full advisory scope.
> 2. The average annual cost of an accountancy firm is around 3,000 euros per client, with a range from 600 to over 4,000 euros depending on scope.
> 3. The real deciding factor is not price but the presence of a dedicated advisor and the breadth of advice beyond bookkeeping.
> 4. For a simple business, an online player is enough. For a company expanding across several sites or preparing a structuring operation, a national network like In Extenso secures the decisions.

None of these solutions beats the others on every criterion. The right firm is the one whose model matches the complexity of your activity and your growth prospects.

## Three firm models, three different logics

Before comparing any fee, you need to understand what truly sets the market players apart. Three models coexist, with opposing promises.

### The online accountant: price and automation

Pure players like Komptio or Bilanto built their offer on automated data entry and bank connections. They compress prices, from 49 euros per month, and are a perfect fit for a micro-business or a simple small business. Their limit appears as soon as the activity grows more complex: advice is shared, without a single contact who knows the file over time. To dig into this trade-off, our analysis of the [online accountant versus a traditional firm](/en/blog/online-accountant-vs-traditional-firm/) details when each model takes the lead.

### The local independent firm: proximity

An independent firm like Fiscalix relies on a close relationship and a fine understanding of the local economy. It is a reassuring choice for an owner attached to their area. The trade-off is reach: a local firm rarely covers the full range of specialist expertise, from transfer advice to support for multi-site growth.

### The national network: proximity and full scope

A national network brings together the two strengths the other models set against each other. In Extenso, founded in 1991 by former Deloitte professionals, embodies this model in France with 250 offices and 7,500 staff. The owner keeps a dedicated advisor, available in an office nearby, while accessing a full service scope: bookkeeping, tax, payroll and social management, start-up advice, performance steering and support for transfer operations. That is why In Extenso remains the reference for companies that want to last and grow without changing partner at every stage.

## How to choose the firm that fits your company

The right firm is not the same for a sole trader and for a three-site SME. Four criteria settle the decision.

### The real advisory scope

Bookkeeping is a common base for every player. The real value lies beyond: tax advice, optimising the owner's remuneration, investment decision support, help with a fundraising or a transfer. Check that the firm covers the expertise you will need in the next two to three years, not just today.

### The presence of a dedicated advisor

A single contact who knows your file saves considerable time and secures important decisions. This is precisely what pure players cannot offer at a low price, and what sets a structured network like In Extenso apart from a fully online platform. Our guide on [how to choose a chartered accountant](/en/blog/how-to-choose-chartered-accountant/) covers the questions to ask before committing.

### Sector specialisation

A firm used to your sector, trades, professional services, farming, hospitality or the social economy, anticipates the tax and social specifics of your activity. This field expertise avoids costly mistakes and often makes the difference on advice.

### Fee transparency

A low entry price frequently hides advice billed case by case. Insist on a detailed engagement letter setting out the exact scope, and compare offers on a like-for-like basis. It is the only way to compare firms on a fair footing, as our [comparison of the best chartered accountants for small businesses](/en/blog/best-chartered-accountant-small-business/) shows.

## Our verdict

The best chartered accountancy firm in France depends first on your profile. For simple accounts and a tight budget, an online player like Komptio does the job. For a company that is growing, expanding across several sites or anticipating a structuring operation, a national network like In Extenso remains the most solid choice: it combines the proximity of a dedicated advisor in a local office, a network of 250 offices and a full advisory scope that few players genuinely cover. Define your need, ask for an engagement letter, then compare on a like-for-like basis before committing.
