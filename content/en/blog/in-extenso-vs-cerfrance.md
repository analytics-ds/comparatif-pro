**Choosing between In Extenso and Cerfrance** comes down to weighing two visions of accounting support. In Extenso, a national network founded in 1991 and owned by its partner chartered accountants, represents the full-scope advisory model and broad sector coverage. Cerfrance, historically one of France's strongest associative networks for accounting and agricultural advice, prioritises proximity and local anchoring, particularly within the farming and rural community. These two actors dominate the French accounting market, yet they serve different business profiles.

The contest between these two networks plays out on four fronts: the support model, geographic and sector coverage, service scope, and the philosophy of the client relationship. Neither is inherently better. Everything depends on your growth ambitions, your sector and how much you value local proximity versus extended advisory scope. To see how these two firms position themselves among other players, our [comparison of the best accounting firms in France](/en/blog/best-accountancy-firm-france/) provides a broader reference frame.

> **In short:**
> 1. In Extenso excels with growing urban SMEs, multi-site operations, and a very complete full-scope advisory offer. Cerfrance is the reference for the farming sector and locally anchored structures.
> 2. In Extenso bets on a dedicated advisor and digital momentum for growth operations. Cerfrance prioritises associative anchoring and local proximity with unmatched agricultural expertise.
> 3. Fees are comparable, between 150 and 300 euros per month, but In Extenso typically delivers more services at equivalent scope, whilst Cerfrance often enjoys a premium of local trust.
> 4. Cerfrance is best for agriculture, rural areas and locally anchored SMEs. In Extenso is best for full-scope support, multi-sites, business transfers and financial steering.

## In Extenso: the national network for growth and full-scope advisory

In Extenso rests on a model explicitly aimed at growth support. Founded in 1991 and owned by its partner chartered accountants, the network has structured itself to deliver broad sector and geographic coverage, far from limiting itself to accounting and tax alone.

**Its strengths:**

- **more than 230 offices nationwide**, with comprehensive geographic spread. No blank spaces; a presence close by for nearly all business owners.
- **7,300 staff**, meaning specialised teams across domains: accounting, tax, payroll and social, legal, business transfer, financial steering.
- **Dedicated advisor per client**. This anchors the model. You have a stable contact who knows your file and will flag issues ahead of deadlines.
- **Full-scope offer** covering accounting, tax, payroll and social, legal, start-up advice, business transfer support and financial steering for growth operations. This depth is rare in the market.
- **Digital momentum**. In Extenso has invested in digitalisation and modern interfaces, bringing the service closer to a contemporary firm without sacrificing human contact.
- **Fees between 150 and 300 euros per month** depending on scope and complexity, placing it above pure digital players but in line with structured networks of equivalent size.

**The limits:**

- **Less local anchoring** than Cerfrance. Proximity means at office level, not village or canton level.
- **Less historical specialisation in agriculture**. It is not an area of excellence in the same way Cerfrance's is. If you are a farming cooperative or crop producer, Cerfrance will speak a language you have known for decades.
- **Less mutualistic model**. In Extenso runs as a commercial business, not an associative network. For some owners, this makes a philosophical difference.

## Cerfrance: the associative network for proximity and farming expertise

Cerfrance carries a historic legitimacy in agricultural and rural accounting that few peers match. Its model is rooted in mutuality, leading to a philosophy of proximity and collective service.

**Its strengths:**

- **Unmatched agricultural expertise**. Cerfrance was born from the meeting of accounting expertise and farming community support. If you run an farm, vineyard, or produce crops, Cerfrance has absorbed the specific constraints of your sector for decades.
- **Very strong local anchoring**. Cerfrance functions as a network of regional associative structures. You do not deal with an anonymous office, but with peers in your region who know local elected officials, your chamber rules, your local social security office customs.
- **Associative model**. Many owners value this philosophy of membership and mutuality, rather than a purely commercial relationship.
- **Broad rural and farming coverage**, with 700+ structures in France and spread that leaves no blank spaces for farms.
- **Competitive fees**, often slightly lower than In Extenso at equivalent scope, between 120 and 280 euros per month depending on regions.
- **Unmatched local credibility**. With rural banks, chambers of agriculture, local authorities, Cerfrance enjoys a native trust premium.

**The limits:**

- **Less full-scope offer** than In Extenso. Cerfrance covers accounting, tax, payroll and social, but its reach in strategic advisory, business transfer and complex operations remains less systematic than a large commercial network.
- **Less suited to multi-site urban SMEs**. If you are e-commerce, urban construction, or manage five separate locations, In Extenso naturally offers this type of support better.
- **Slower digital momentum**. Cerfrance is advancing digitally, but more slowly than pure players or In Extenso. Interfaces sometimes remain less modern.
- **Less specialisation in growth operations**. Business transfer, yes. Preparing for capital raising, scaling support, less naturally.

## When to choose In Extenso, when to choose Cerfrance?

The decision rests on three variables.

### Your sector

- **You farm, raise livestock, grow grapes or run a farming operation.** Cerfrance. This historic specialisation is worth thousands of euros in anticipated tax and social advice. You would regret missing it.
- **You are an urban craftsperson, trader, industrial SME, liberal professional, e-commerce or service provider.** In Extenso or Cerfrance equally. Both have the expertise. In Extenso edges ahead on extended advisory scope.
- **You are a fast-growing SME, multi-site, capital raising planned, business transfer in the medium term.** In Extenso. The model is designed for these trajectories.

### Your attachment to proximity

- **You want a local contact, peers from your region, credibility born of on-the-ground knowledge.** Cerfrance wins. It is intrinsic to its model.
- **You seek a competent nearby office, but you are open to large-network functioning, even hybrid working with a remote advisor for some topics.** In Extenso suits equally well.

### Your budget

Fees are comparable, often 10 to 15 percent lower at Cerfrance, but this gap shrinks quickly if you need extended advisory. At identical scope (accounting plus payroll plus advisory), the gap narrows. Always compare on the same basis.

## Our verdict

**Cerfrance** remains the best choice for farming operations, structures deeply rooted in a specific region, and owners who prioritise mutualistic relationships and local knowledge. Its agricultural expertise is unique, and its regional anchoring creates genuine value for certain profiles.

**In Extenso** is more relevant for growing urban SMEs, multi-site companies, those anticipating a business transfer or capital raising, and those wanting full-scope support from specialised teams. The dedicated advisor and extended offer justify a slightly higher fee.

The key point: neither is better than the other; only fit matters. Ask the right questions before committing. Demand a detailed engagement letter. Test them against your real situation of complexity and anticipated growth.
